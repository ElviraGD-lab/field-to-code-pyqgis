title: "From Field to Code: PyQGIS from Scratch"
---

Build a real tool and learn to think like an engineer.

This book follows a single real problem from start to finish: which homes stand close to overhead power lines, and how long help takes to reach them. Along the way you will learn Python and PyQGIS, but above all you will learn to think like an engineer: turn a concern into a question, look for the right tool, check every step and arrive at a result you can trust.
